    About this page

    Everything is in this one file: nothing is downloaded or sent anywhere. It holds the Stump @@RELEASE@@, MicroPython @@MICROPYTHON@@ (MIT licence, micropython.org) for the ESP32-S3 with octal PSRAM, and esptool-js (Apache-2.0, Espressif), which writes it. Each file's SHA-256 is checked on the board after it's copied. Settings are written by the Stump's own settings.py, as from /admin.
